---
title: Direction Arrays
---

- Repeated movement → use `dx[]`, `dy[]`.
- Avoid manually writing every direction.
- Coordinate → `pair<int,int>`.
- Unique coordinates → `set<pair<int,int>>`.
- Think: **"What changes between the cases?"**

```cpp
int dx[4] = {-1, 1, -1, 1}
int dy[4] = {-1, -1, 1, 1};
```
